---
title: LeanCPM vs Lean
---
Lean is a production management approach for construction, adapted from lean manufacturing.

### The good
Lean is simple—no need for CPM theory. Anyone can list tasks on a board or with Post-it notes.

### The bad

Its major flaw is that it’s not parametric, making plans quickly useless once conditions change. Relationships are inferred, so deviations require reworking plans. This frustrates many and hinders Lean from replacing CPM.

### The ugly

Construction differs greatly from manufacturing, which Lean is based on. While reducing DOWNTIME is valuable, finishing on time or early is often more important. 

PPC (Planned Percent Complete) is the measure of how many tasks that were set out to be completed are actually completed, and doesn’t take into consideration high-priority work. Missing 20% is a good thing, even if the 20% were all critical activities.

In Lean, all tasks are treated with the same sense of urgency, which should not be the case.

### The difference

The idea behind LeanCPM is that it enables anyone to create a plan, too, albeit with a slightly different (but improved) interface. In the process, whether they know it or not, they’re also creating dependencies between tasks, making a logically sound plan.

LeanCPM is to CPM what Wix and Squarespace are to HTML/CSS. It’s not quite drawing boxes on a screen, but it’s close, and it’s responsive to different screen sizes, or in our case, different outcomes with task progress.
